Help & Support

Welcome to the Lighthouse Helpdesk help section. Here you’ll find guidance on using the platform, managing customers, products, renewals, and your user profile.

Getting Started

After logging in, use the navigation bar at the top to access your Dashboard, Renewals, Products, and Customers. Each section provides tools to manage your company’s data.

Managing Customers

Go to Customers to add, edit, or view customer details. Each customer page also shows all renewals linked to that customer.

Managing Products

The Products section lets you maintain your product catalogue, including cost, RRP, and descriptions. Product details automatically appear when creating or editing renewals.

Renewals

The Renewals page lists all active renewals. You can create new renewals, edit existing ones, and archive completed or cancelled renewals.

User Profile & MFA

Visit Profile to update your personal details, phone number, avatar, and MFA settings. Administrators can manage users and reset passwords.

Need More Help?

Contact your company administrator or reach out via the Support link in the footer.
